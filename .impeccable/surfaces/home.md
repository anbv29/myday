# Homepage
Mode: Operate. Scope: homepage and shared frontend typography, surfaces, navigation.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A collection of submitted dates, not a conventional month calendar.
OWN-WORLD: Warm white, peach selected surfaces, charcoal text, clean Manrope typography,
quiet borders, restrained motion, and a functional chronological date collection.
STORY: Browse submitted dates, inspect paid values and claimant stories, then register
for free or continue to existing paid checkout. Paid takes priority over free.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact headline, paid/free entry paths, collection filters and sorting,
then actual submitted date cards. Chronology includes year; highest-paid sorting is optional.
Mobile stacks records and wraps controls without ordinary empty calendar-day cells.
FORM: User-pinned calendar-first direction, approved in chat. concept-seed attempted,
but engine 0.1.5 unavailable; no generated assignment or seed key is claimed.
SIGNATURE: Real dates carry their story, public attribution, and paid/free status together.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review,
the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Implementation path: code-first fallback, not a stored global workflow preference.
No new shipping raster assets are required for this functional calendar surface.
