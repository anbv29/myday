# MYDAY

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Visitors browse meaningful dates and the people or organisations claiming them.
Buyers attach a story and public attribution to a date without creating an account.

## Product Purpose
A public date collection with free registrations and paid, verified featured claims.
One current claim is shown for a date; a higher valid claim can replace it.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve existing checkout, payment verification, Supabase, Redis, routes, and API contracts.
Free registration is an isolated additive feature in existing Supabase, not a new architecture.
Do not change existing paid checkout contracts or invent availability, payments, bidding activity,
testimonials, ownership guarantees, or financial returns.
The homepage list is a limited public showcase, not the complete database.

## Brand Commitments
Name: MYDAY. User-approved direction: calendar-first, cool silver and graphite with electric teal and mint accents.
Retain working light and dark themes and visible claimant attribution.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing public-data adapters and date-detail routes supply real claim content.
Development preview has a labelled October 29, 2026 example for @anewbhev at $10.

## Product Principles
Find a date before processing promotional content.
Explain replacement rules before checkout.
Show the date, claimant, story, and value without fabricated social proof.
Keep account-free browsing and checkout.
The first free entry is visible until a paid claim takes priority for that exact date/year.
Free entries never join the payment-ranked leaderboard or imply a completed payment.
